Every run found 664579 primes below 10,000,000. On this Apple Silicon macOS
machine, real times for 1, 2, 4, 10 and 100 threads were 1.75, 0.78, 0.41,
0.16 and 0.06 seconds. More threads ran independent subranges in parallel.
The interval division gives each thread a similar number of integers, but
upper subranges cost more because trial division takes longer there. Timings
are single measurements and depend on machine load and scheduling.
