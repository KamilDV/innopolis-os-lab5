Every run found 664579 primes. Real times for 1, 2, 4, 10 and 100 threads
were 1.60, 1.14, 1.29, 1.26 and 0.73 seconds, compared with 1.75, 0.78,
0.41, 0.16 and 0.06 seconds for exercise 3. Each worker reserves a unique
candidate under the mutex, tests it without holding the lock, then locks
again only to count a prime. This keeps the expensive test parallel and
ensures no candidate is skipped or duplicated. The two lock operations per
candidate cause contention, especially with many threads. The less uniform
work distribution sometimes offsets part of that cost. Measurements are
single runs on macOS and are sensitive to scheduling.
