The slide says to implement exercise 3 with semaphores instead of condition
variables, although exercise 3 has no condition variables. This solution
interprets that as a reference to the producer-consumer exercise 5 and
replaces its condition variables with counting semaphores. All five runs
found 664579 primes. Real times for 1, 2, 4, 10 and 100 consumers were
6.80, 6.94, 10.75, 11.77 and 11.65 seconds. This is slower than exercise 3
in every measured case and slower than exercise 5 up to 10 consumers. The
sem_wait/sem_post operations on every candidate and shared queue contention
are substantial. Named semaphores are unlinked immediately after creation
for compatibility with macOS, where sem_init is unavailable.
