Every run found 664579 primes. Real times for 1, 2, 4, 10 and 100 consumers
were 2.56, 1.92, 1.75, 4.20 and 24.02 seconds. Exercise 3's times were
1.75, 0.78, 0.41, 0.16 and 0.06 seconds; exercise 4's were 1.60, 1.14,
1.29, 1.26 and 0.73 seconds. The bounded queue and condition variables
coordinate one producer and multiple consumers. Values divisible by 2 or 3
are discarded before queuing, with 2 and 3 counted separately. At high thread
counts, contention and wakeups dominate the relatively cheap primality checks.
These are single macOS measurements, not stable performance guarantees.
